Quick Safety Tips

👀

Awareness

Keep your head up, scan exits, and trust your instincts.

Escape First

Your priority is to create distance and move to safety — not to fight.

Make Noise

Use your voice to attract attention — shout firm commands, not whispers.

Basic Techniques

Wrist Release

Rotate and step to leverage your weight; free your hand and run to safety.

Low Kick

Target shins or knee joint to break contact and create an opportunity to escape.

Palm Strike

Strike with the heel of the palm to the nose or chin for quick disruption.

Emergency Help

If you feel threatened, call local authorities immediately.
