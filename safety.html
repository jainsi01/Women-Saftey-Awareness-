Safety Tips

Stay Aware, Stay Safe

Small precautions can make a big difference

🚶 Stay Alert

Always be aware of your surroundings, especially in public places.

📍 Share Location

Share your live location with trusted friends or family members.

🌙 Avoid Isolation

Avoid deserted or poorly lit areas, especially at night.

📱 Phone Ready

Keep your phone charged and emergency numbers on speed dial.

💡 Trust Instincts

If something feels wrong, trust your instincts and seek help.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Inform Someone

Let someone know where you are going and when you’ll return.
